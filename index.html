05 / Common research questions

AI in digital forensics: FAQs

How is artificial intelligence used in digital forensics?

AI can assist investigators by classifying digital artifacts, detecting anomalies, prioritizing evidence, correlating events, and helping analyze memory, networks, mobile devices, and multimedia. Human validation remains essential.

Can AI-generated forensic findings be used as evidence?

Admissibility depends on the jurisdiction, methods, validation, and case context. AI output alone is not a guarantee of evidentiary reliability; provenance, error rates, transparency, and expert review matter.

What are the major limitations of AI in digital forensics?

Common concerns include limited representative datasets, poor reproducibility, domain shift, explainability, adversarial manipulation, privacy, and legal defensibility.

Where can I read a systematic review of AI-driven digital forensics?

The open-access 2026 systematic review by Islam and coauthors is available at DOI: 10.3390/digital6040094.
